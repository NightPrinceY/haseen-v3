# Source 02: HadiPTUK/kinship_dialect (checked 2026-10-13)

- Repo: https://github.com/HadiPTUK/kinship_dialect (1 file, no licence, last push 2023-05-25). Local copy:
  `data/sources/kinship/kinship_indonesian_arabic_supplement.xlsx`.
- Paper: Khalilia et al., "Lexical Diversity in Kinship Across Languages and Dialects", arXiv 2308.13056.
- Content: 254 kinship concepts × 11 columns (Indonesian, Javanese, Banjar, MSA, Gulf, Moroccan, Algerian, Tunisian,
  Palestinian, Egyptian, Syrian). Only 106 concepts have any Arabic form; 73–99 filled cells per variety.
- Quality: many cells repeat the MSA form in every dialect (حفيد، شقيق، أخ لأب، ابن الأخ), i.e. "the concept is
  lexicalised", not "this is what people say". A few real dialect items: أبيه / أبلة (Egyptian), ستي (Syrian),
  ابو العود / ام العود (Gulf), باب الشيخ (Algerian), ولد خويا (Maghrebi).
- Author's other repos: food terms are English↔MSA only; kinship for Persian/Turkish/Maba; no further dialect data.

Verdict: very low value for V2. Single domain (kinship), about 30 genuinely dialectal forms, no examples, no
negative evidence. At most a handful of probe items for a test list. Not used for training.
